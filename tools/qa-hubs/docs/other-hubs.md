# Other major-center coverage

Feros' Zhu's Hope is the next dense colony target after Citadel/Noveria. Virmire's allied camp adds a major
outdoor staging area with actors and scenery. Their catalog manifests are intentionally draft: no coordinates
or native traversal evidence have been invented. Local package names (`BIOA_WAR00.pcc`, `BIOA_JUG00.pcc`)
were checked read-only on 2026-11-08. [Research/59](../../../research/59-conversations-in-world.md) supplies
the setting/prefix mapping and records that direct map-open attempts did not reach gameplay on these worlds.

The next native step is to identify a real played save at a safe on-foot point, load it through the normal game
flow, record the start/facing and short waypoints, and restore the complete save/configuration backup afterward.
Only then can a route become ready. One successful controller walk supplies a route receipt; repeated clean
captures with the same save/build/settings supply regression evidence. None of those gates is satisfied merely
by package presence, static streaming data, a manually opened persistent level or a lower observed maximum.

Therum and Ilos are potential later traversal/render targets, but are not initially presented as social hubs.
Their arrival/plot transitions require separate fixture work. Keep any fixture contents private and only publish
the scripts, manifest constraints and compact status/evidence references.
