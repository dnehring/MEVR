# What the player gets

- X held on foot opens a wheel of the player's powers at the left palm. Moving the left hand toward a tile (or the left
  stick) picks it; letting go of X equips it. A tap of X keeps the wheel open until a pinch, a squeeze or X again.
- With a power equipped and the left arm raised, the enemy the left hand points at is locked and marked. A push of the
  open left hand along its ray casts the power at it. The right hand keeps the gun.
- Powers that aim at nothing are cast by a gesture of the open left hand: the palm on the chest, a slap on the gun, the
  hand above the head.
- The right stick click held 0.3 s opens the game's own power wheel (pause and aim, squad powers); a tap casts nothing.
- A pull of the left trigger opens the game's weapon wheel at the left hand; the right stick steers it; letting go
  gives the highlighted weapon. Pointing the left hand at a squadmate while pulling puts the wheel on their weapon.
- A free left grip squeeze points a squad order: let go on enemies = attack, on the ground = move, near yourself = follow.
- The right B held throws an arc from the right hand; let go and the squad (or the squadmate picked on a small wheel)
  goes to the ring and holds. B double-tapped calls everyone back.
- The left grip squeezed at the left ear is the radio: speak an order, let go.
- Thespian mode (off by default): read a dialogue reply aloud and it is taken.
- Shepard's forearm wears the game's omni-tool while the wheel is open and around tech casts.

# Arbitration

The left grip, the left trigger, X and B each have more than one taker on foot. The order is the mod's tick order in
[Mod.cpp](../../../src/mod/Mod.cpp) (holsters, power wheel, squad weapons, throwables, melee, vent, support grip, squad
context, voice, squad orders, squad placement) plus each module's own exclusions.

Where the prose and the code differ (the code is what this file states):

- `core/vr/VoiceGesture.hpp`'s header says the radio claims its squeeze before the support grip and the grenade slot.
  `game/Voice.cpp` gives a grenade grab and the vent the squeeze first (`!throwables::ClaimedLeft() && !vent::ClaimedLeft()`);
  only the flat orders come after the radio, as `CLAUDE.md` says.
- `CLAUDE.md` gives the player's power release as about 0.58 s after the cast; the code's default before a delay is
  learned is 0.55 s (`kDefaultReleaseS`).
- The lock's 1.5 s wind-up (`kWindUpS`) and the push's 0.4 s wait after a left grip are in the code only.
- `LockSettings::on`'s comment still says that with the lock off the right stick click's tap casts; since 2026-11-05
  a tap casts nothing (PWR-23).
- `squad_menu::wheel::Panel::powerPlace` defaults to 1 (right hand) in the core struct; the setting the mod publishes
  is `powerWheelAt = hud` (-1).
