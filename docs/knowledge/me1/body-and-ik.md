# What the player gets

- Shepard's arms follow the controllers: each hand is where the controller is, the elbow bends naturally, the gun
  sits in the right hand and points where the controller aims.
- Looking down, the body is under the head: it turns with the player, leans with the head and steps after it. While
  the stick walks, the legs keep running the way Shepard runs and only the spine twists toward the view.
- The body is the player's size, and the eyes are at the player's real height above the game's floor, whatever the
  recenter, the animation or the camera mode did. Crouching for real lowers the view once.
- Fingers curl with the trigger and the grip; on Touch and Index controllers a lifted index points and a lifted
  thumb goes up.
- A drawn weapon does not change the eye point, the walk follows the stick within a tenth of a second, half a push
  walks, and the draw animation is six times as fast.
- A guided calibration of about a minute (stand, T pose, arms ahead, crouch or "I play seated", a pistol orb and two
  rifle orbs aimed at three targets, a red-dot check) measures the body and the gun stances per controller type.
- The body never drags the camera with it, and it does not vanish at head angles where the game's own camera would
  cull it.

| Legs: standing rule or the run | The run while the pawn moves above 60 units/s (BOD-10) | The standing hip turn fades in 0.25 s |
| Legs in a scripted walk in the world | `worldLegs = stand` while the view rests, the script's walk while it moves | See [research/69](../../../research/69-conversations-everywhere.md) "The legs under the player" |
| Eye height: floor lock or the constant eye | The floor lock where it applies; `cameraOffsetUp`'s vertical part is absorbed | The constant eye still sets horizontal placement |
| Crouch: the real head or L3 | A physical crouch sets `ShouldCrouch` on physical changes only; L3 toggles in between; neither feeds the standing references | Seated: physical crouch off |
| The whole pad while calibrating | The calibration | The game sees a neutral pad, the mod no controllers; no holsters, grenades, melee, squad, grip, crouch |
| Write order inside the hook | Stance pre-stage (weights), compose, arm graft, torso, support grip, gun kick, arms, fingers, legs, scale | A later stage reads the earlier one's result |
